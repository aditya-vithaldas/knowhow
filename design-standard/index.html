Type & hierarchy

Manrope 700 for confident, gently rounded headings. DM Sans 400–600 for readable body copy and controls. Body text starts at 16px. Navy carries the content; violet identifies the changing phrase.

Shape & illustration

10px controls, 12px cards, fine blue-gray borders. Pencil grain, broken contours and crosshatching belong in the illustrations. Keep the interface crisp and shadows faint.

Motion standard

Type at roughly 55ms per character, hold for 3 seconds, erase at 25ms. Fade the matching illustration in as each new question begins. Reserve the headline height. Reduced motion shows a static question.
