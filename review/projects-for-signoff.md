## 1. Voice-first e-commerce shopping

Live page: https://knowhow.build/projects/voice-commerce/

| Field | Current content |
|---|---|
| Card title | Voice-first e-commerce shopping |
| One-line description | Search, ask about products and shortlist them in one continuous voice conversation. |
| Category / filter | Commerce / E-commerce |
| Hero question | Has anybody already built a shop you can just talk to? |
| Builder label | Commerce product builder (name blurred) |

**The problem**
> What happens when shopping becomes a conversation instead of a stack of filters?

**Thought process**
> Voice is great for working out what you want, but you still need to see products, specs and reviews. The conversation and the screen have to work together.

**The solution**
> Let people ramble about what they need: budget, use, compromises. A live voice model listens and speaks at the same time, while the screen shows matching products, details, sample reviews and a growing shortlist.

## 4. Proactive analytics: insights without asking

Live page: https://knowhow.build/projects/proactive-analytics/

| Field | Current content |
|---|---|
| Card title | Proactive analytics: insights without asking |
| One-line description | Benchmarks, data-hygiene checks and a daily brief instead of another dashboard. |
| Category / filter | Analytics / E-commerce |
| Hero question | Has anybody already built analytics that tell you what changed before you ask? |
| Builder label | E-commerce analytics lead (name blurred) |

**The problem**
> Most e-commerce firms don’t need another dashboard. How do they find out what moved, why, and what to do about it?

**Thought process**
> An opinionated harness gives clear answers but must be open about its assumptions, and it improves only as people feed back the “why” behind the numbers.

**The solution**
> Package domain knowledge into a thin agent harness: benchmark against comparable businesses, flag the data-hygiene gaps that block better decisions, learn what normal looks like and surface only meaningful deviations each morning, and build a shared company brain in Slack and MCP.

## 5. Voice-driven seller onboarding: scan your shelf, say what to sell

Live page: https://knowhow.build/projects/voice-seller-onboarding/

| Field | Current content |
|---|---|
| Card title | Voice-driven seller onboarding: scan your shelf, say what to sell |
| One-line description | One photo of a shelf becomes item cards with prices and demand; the seller picks what to sell by voice. |
| Category / filter | Commerce / E-commerce |
| Hero question | Has anybody already built seller onboarding where you just say what to sell? |
| Builder label | Marketplace product builder (name blurred) |

**The problem**
> C2C marketplaces can’t switch on supply when they need it. People already own the items buyers want, but cataloguing each one costs more effort than a few euros is worth.

**Thought process**
> It’s never one product at a time. A bookshelf or an electronics cabinet is a bulk job, so start from one photo. And go where the seller already is: an occasional seller won’t install an app, but will send photos in a chat.

**The solution**
> Take one photo of a shelf. The AI separates the items into cards with a title, an estimated price and a demand signal. The seller just says “sell this, sell this”, or taps. The live demo covers scanning, estimates and selection; chat-app integration, publishing and pickup are next.
